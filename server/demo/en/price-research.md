## Findings
- Market range: $0.90 to $1.60; most shops sit between $1.15 and $1.40.
- Cheaper competitors offer no delivery; pricier ones sell the atmosphere.

## Recommendation
- Launch price $1.00 (16 oz), then raise to $1.20 after the second week.
- Stress "real palm sugar" and delivery as differentiators.
